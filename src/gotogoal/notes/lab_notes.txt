Step 3
Which topic carries the robot's odometry, and what is its exact message type?
The odometry topic is /odom and the message type is nav_msgs/msg/Odometry.

Inside that message, which fields hold the robot's position, and which field holds its orientation?
What is the name of the frame the pose is expressed in, and what is the name of the frame
attached to the robot?
The position is stored in pose.pose.position.x, pose.pose.position.y, and pose.pose.position.z.
The orientation is stored in pose.pose.orientation. The pose is expressed in the odom frame, and
the frame attached to the robot is base_link.

In what form is the orientation given? Is it an angle?
The orientation is given as a quaternion with x, y, z, and w values. It is not given directly as one
angle.
